American football is a team sport played between two teams.

The offense attempts to advance the ball toward the opponent's
end zone by running or passing.

A touchdown is worth six points. After a touchdown, the team
can attempt a one-point kick or a two-point conversion.

A field goal is worth three points.

The quarterback is usually responsible for passing the ball
and coordinating the offense.

Wide receivers primarily run routes and catch passes from
the quarterback.
